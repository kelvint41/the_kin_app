# Community Events Board Strategy

**Mission**: Build solidarity in the Black community by showcasing collective action. Businesses share events, partnerships, and initiatives—not competing, but **collaborating for community wealth**.

**Core Insight**: A job board helps individuals find work. A community board helps businesses *work together* for shared values.

---

## The Problem We Solve

- ❌ Backpack drives happen, but only local networks know
- ❌ Businesses want to partner but have no platform
- ❌ Community initiatives stay siloed (one business, one neighborhood)
- ❌ Black-owned businesses rarely see each other's community work
- ❌ National Black business events are invisible to local owners

**Solution**: **Community Events Board** — A place where Black-owned businesses announce, coordinate, and celebrate collective impact.

---

## Features

### **For Business Owners (Event Posting)**

✅ **Post Events**
- Event title, type (service/partnership/celebration), date/time, location
- Description (what, why, who it helps)
- Invite specific businesses as co-organizers
- "Looking for partners?" toggle (search + invite businesses)
- How people can help (donate, volunteer, partner, sponsor)
- Banner image (visual impact)

✅ **Manage Event**
- View interested/confirmed participants
- Message co-organizers + volunteers
- Update event (date change, needs change)
- Archive completed event
- Celebrate impact (photos, testimonials)

✅ **Collaborate**
- Add partner businesses as co-organizers
- Tag other businesses in event
- Co-marketing (post to Exchange too)
- Share credit equally

### **For Community Members (Event Discovery)**

✅ **Browse Events**
- Filter by type (service, partnership, celebration)
- Filter by location (local, regional, national)
- Filter by date (this week, this month, upcoming)
- Search by keyword
- Trending/popular events

✅ **Participate**
- Register interest or commitment
- Volunteer time (select dates/roles)
- Donate supplies (link to wishlist)
- Donate funds (if applicable)
- Share event with network

✅ **Track Impact**
- See event status (upcoming, in progress, completed)
- View how many helped
- Read thank yous/testimonials
- Download certificate of participation (if volunteer)

## Tier-Based Features

### **Free Tier**
- ✅ Browse all events
- ✅ Volunteer/participate
- ✅ Post 1 event per month
- ✅ Comment/interact
- ❌ No event promotion
- ❌ Limited to local events only

### **Founder Tier ($29)**
- ✅ Post unlimited events
- ✅ Partner search + invitations
- ✅ View volunteer/participant list
- ✅ Message participants
- ❌ No featured placement
- ✅ Access to regional events

### **Founding Local+ ($59+)**
- ✅ Everything from Founder +
- ✅ Featured event placement (rotate in "Featured Events")
- ✅ Event promotion badge
- ✅ Analytics (interest, participation)
- ✅ Access to national events

### **Premium Local+ ($99+)**
- ✅ Everything from Founding Local +
- ✅ Promoted event placement (premium rotation)
- ✅ "Trusted Organizer" badge
- ✅ Detailed participation analytics
- ✅ Co-organizer collaboration tools

### **Elite ($149+)**
- ✅ Everything from Premium Local +
- ✅ **Featured event showcase** (prominent top spot)
- ✅ "Community Leader" badge
- ✅ Advanced partner matching AI
- ✅ Event impact reporting dashboard
- ✅ Co-marketing support

## Why This Matters

✅ **Builds solidarity**: Businesses see each other's community work
✅ **Prevents silos**: One event becomes network events
✅ **Attracts mission-driven owners**: "KIN cares about community"
✅ **National visibility**: Local event can inspire national movement
✅ **Drives tier upgrades**: "Need to reach more partners? Upgrade to Founder+"
✅ **Creates stickiness**: People return to volunteer, support causes
✅ **Generates content**: Exchange gets retweets of events, testimonials
✅ **Documents impact**: Celebrate wins (kids helped, funds raised, lives changed)

**Result**: KIN becomes the hub for Black business community action, not just commerce.

```

    ↓
Community grows, businesses prosper together

National recognition: "KIN Businesses Help 5,000 Kids"
    ↓
```

## Summary

**Community Events Board = Community Infrastructure**

This isn't a feature to extract money—it's a feature to **bind the community together**. Every event posted strengthens the network. Every partnership created raises the tide for all boats.

When Black businesses see each other succeeding *and supporting their community*, they want to join that movement. That's your conversion engine. 🚀
