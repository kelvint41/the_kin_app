Questions owners ask

Is the listing really free?

Yes. The Community tier — listing, ticker, KIN Score, reviews, and discovery — is free, permanently. Paid tiers add growth tools like the AI marketing studio, longer Power Hours, and priority placement, but visibility is never paywalled.

What does "verified" mean on KIN?

It means a human reviewed your listing and confirmed you're the owner. Verification is layered: owner-submitted listings show as self-reported until you add supporting evidence (a business registration or established social profile), which earns the gold Verified badge.

My business is already listed. Where did that come from?

Community members can suggest businesses they love, and our launch directory seeded some public listings. Claim yours in the app to take control of the profile, and we'll verify you as the owner.

What is the KIN Score?

A live engagement metric — visits, reviews, and connections move it up. It's how the community's support becomes visible. It is not a credit score or a financial product.

I'm not in San Antonio. Can I still join?

Yes — register now and you'll be in line for your city. Houston and Atlanta are next, and early registrations launch with a head start on the Exchange.
