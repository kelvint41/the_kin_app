## Feature Interaction Flow

### **Customer Journey (Paths to Engagement)**

```
1. DISCOVERY PATH
   Download KIN

     ↓

     ↓

     ↓
   See other customers on KIN Quest
     ↓

     ↓

     ↓

     ↓

     ↓
```

## How Features Reinforce Each Other

### **Map + Location Beacon + Carousel**
- Map shows all businesses
- Location Beacon active? → Carousel highlights them
- Customer sees real-time positioning → drives conversion
- Premium carousel placement → incentive to upgrade tier

### **Exchange + Community Events**
- Owner posts promotion on Exchange
- Also posts community event on Events Board
- Customers see both sides: "business + values"
- Community respects businesses that give back

### **Job Board + Business Growth**
- Business finds employees via Job Board
- New hire uses KIN Quest
- Friend of hire joins platform
- Referral loop expands network

### **KIN Quest + Community Discovery**
- Customer explores businesses (Quest)
- Finds backpack drive (Community Board)
- Volunteers at event
- Meets business owner
- Becomes loyal customer
- Cycle repeats

### **App Studio + Job Board**
- Entrepreneur builds app via Studio
- Launches business
- Posts jobs on Job Board
- Hires employees
- Grows business
- Becomes community leader
- Posts community events
- More people join KIN to work with them

## How Tiers Drive Engagement at Every Layer

### **Free Tier ($0)**
- Discover (map, showcase, quest)
- Engage (follow, review, post on Exchange)
- Participate (volunteer, apply for jobs, join events)
- **Motivation**: Everything else is free; consider upgrading

### **Founder ($29/month)**
- Everything from Free +
- Location Beacon feature (broadcast your location)
- Post unlimited jobs
- In-app messaging
- **Motivation**: "I have customers; time to hire"

### **Founding Local ($59/month)**
- Everything from Founder +
- Regular carousel visibility (1-2x/week)
- Advanced analytics
- Community event organization
- **Motivation**: "I'm established; ready to lead"

### **Premium Local ($99/month)**
- Everything from Founding Local +
- Premium carousel placement (3-4x/week)
- Featured event promotion
- Partner matching tools
- **Motivation**: "I'm scaling; ready to invest"

### **Elite ($149/month)**
- Everything from Premium Local +
- Always featured (daily carousel)
- Community leader badge
- Premium partner network
- Co-marketing opportunities
- **Motivation**: "I'm the flagship; let's lead together"

## Why This Works

✅ **No feature stands alone**
- Job board drives tier upgrades (better hiring)
- Tier upgrades fund community initiatives
- Community events attract more users
- More users = more business discovery
- Business discovery = more spending
- More spending = more jobs posted
- More jobs = more tier upgrades

✅ **Aligned incentives**
- Users: Free to browse, pay only for premium visibility
- Business owners: Free to list, pay for growth tools + marketing
- KIN: Revenue from those scaling, not from desperation pricing
- Community: Wins from more jobs, more collaboration, more wealth circulation

✅ **Mission-driven growth**
- Not extracting from community, building community
- Every feature asks: "Does this help Black businesses succeed?"
- Revenue is byproduct of helping, not driver of features

✅ **Network effects**
- Each new user adds value to existing users (more discoveries, more jobs)
- Each new business adds value (more jobs, more events, more collaboration)
- Exponential growth without paid ads (word-of-mouth built in)
